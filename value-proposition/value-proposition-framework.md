# 💡 AI Prompts: Value Proposition Framework

## 🎯 Objective
Generate a powerful, differentiated, customer-centric value proposition using proven frameworks (Value Proposition Canvas, StoryBrand, Made to Stick).

## 📋 Quick-Start Power Prompt
```text
You're an expert-level Product Marketing Manager with 20+ years of experience across SaaS, marketplaces, and B2B. Your task is to craft a powerful, strategic value proposition that clearly communicates the product's unique value to the target customer, aligned with real-world customer insights and competitive differentiation.
🧠 Detailed Strategic Prompt
Plaintext
You're an expert-level Product Marketing Manager with 20+ years of experience across SaaS, marketplaces, and B2B. Your task is to craft a powerful, strategic value proposition that clearly communicates the product's unique value to the target customer, aligned with real-world customer insights and competitive differentiation.

To deliver the highest quality output, begin by asking up to 5 sharp, insight-driven questions to clarify:
1. The target customer or ICP (job role, industry, company size, pain points)
2. The primary problem or friction the product solves
3. The most meaningful outcome or transformation for the customer
4. Key differentiators from competitors or alternatives
5. Real-world quotes, testimonials, or customer data that show product impact

Once you gather this context, apply the following frameworks:
- Value Proposition Canvas (Gains, Pains, Jobs)
- StoryBrand (Customer → Problem → Guide → Plan → Success)
- Made to Stick principles (Simple, Unexpected, Concrete, Credible, Emotional, Story-based)

Then generate the following outputs:
1. One-line value proposition: A headline-style, benefit-oriented statement.
2. Expanded version (2–3 sentences): Usable for decks, websites, or demos.
3. Customer voice rewrite: Restate the value from the customer's POV.
4. Optional tagline or slogan: Short, memorable phrase usable in marketing.
5. 3 Reasons to Believe (RTBs): Supportive proof points.
