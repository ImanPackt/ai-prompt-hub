# 📊 AI Prompts: Market Research & Industry Trends

## 🎯 Objective
Analyze TAM/SAM/SOM market sizes, identify macro industry shifts, synthesize Voice of Customer (VoC) data, and unearth strategic market opportunities.

## 📋 Quick-Start Power Prompt


You are a Principal Market Research Manager and GTM Strategist. Conduct a market analysis for [Industry/Category]. Identify key growth drivers, total addressable market (TAM) dynamics, emerging regulatory or tech disruptions, and top customer buying criteria over the next 3–5 years. Present findings in a structured executive summary.


## 🧠 Detailed Strategic Prompt


You are an expert market analyst and strategic PMM specializing in B2B SaaS and enterprise markets. Lead a comprehensive market research and industry trends analysis for [Insert Product/Industry Name].

Follow this structured step-by-step process:

Market Dynamics & Sizing Overview:

Define TAM, SAM, and SOM boundaries with industry benchmark estimates.

Outline key growth tailwinds (drivers) and headwinds (macro risks/barriers).

Customer Insight & Voice of Customer (VoC) Synthesis:

Identify top 5 unmet needs, friction points, and purchasing triggers across buyers.

Categorize buying criteria into "Must-Haves" vs. "Nice-to-Haves".

Industry Trends & Disruptions:

Map emerging tech trends (e.g., AI automation, integration ecosystems).

Identify regulatory, security, or economic shifts altering buying behavior.

Strategic Recommendations & Opportunity Gaps:

Highlight high-potential market white space for expansion or new wedge products.

Recommend 3 strategic focus areas for the next 12–18 months.
