# ⚔️ AI Prompts: Competitive Research & Positioning

## 🎯 Objective
Analyze competitors, identify market white space, and construct sharp competitive battlecards and positioning matrices.

## 📋 Quick-Start Power Prompt

You are an expert competitive intelligence analyst and Product Marketing Director. Conduct a comprehensive competitive teardown between [My Product/Company] and [Competitor Name]. Identify core feature gaps, pricing/packaging differences, primary target segments, and positioning strategies. Present findings in a structured battlecard format with clear win/loss talk tracks.


## 🧠 Detailed Strategic Prompt

You are an expert competitive intelligence analyst and Product Marketing Manager. Lead a deep-dive competitive positioning exercise for [Insert Product Name/Industry].

Follow this structured step-by-step process:

Category & Competitor Identification:

Identify the top 3 direct competitors and 2 indirect/substitute alternatives.

Map each competitor's primary wedge strategy and target customer segment.

Feature & Capability Differentiation:

Build a competitive matrix comparing core capabilities across 4 dimensions: Ease of Use, Enterprise Readiness, Speed to Value, and Customization.

Highlight our product's unfair advantage ("Why Us") vs. competitor gaps.

Pricing & Packaging Analysis:

Compare entry-tier pricing, expansion metrics (per seat, per usage), and value-gated features.

Identify potential pricing friction points or competitive displacement opportunities.

Sales Battlecard & Objection Handling:

Provide 3 primary objection handling scripts ("When the prospect says X about Competitor Y, reply with Z").

Define key land-and-expand strategies for displacing incumbents.
